Design system

A preview of tokens.css. Change a value there, reload, and you see it applied here — there are no copied values on this page.

To decide on a palette: edit the :root blocks in tokens.css —the light one and the two dark ones— and reload. The contrasts below recompute themselves: if one comes out red, that combination does not meet WCAG AA and is not used for text.

Palette and contrast

Each token over its -content. The ratio is measured in the browser over the already-resolved color, so it reflects the active theme.
